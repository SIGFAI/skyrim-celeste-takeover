# Celeste Takeover

**Skyrim** × Celeste · visual · idea by the team

Bring Celeste into the game in the most visible and funny way. The change shows in the first 30 seconds of play.

> I put Celeste in Skyrim: giant strawberries orbit me, Madelines dash-fight Badeline in the snow, and I Fus Ro Dah the berry rain.

## Install

Copy the contents of the release folder (Sigf.esp, Scripts, Meshes, Textures) into your Skyrim Special Edition Data folder and tick Sigf.esp in the launcher. Load any save: snow starts, strawberries circle you, and Madelines and Badeline fight nearby.
